Chime Detector Simulator WASM · core/

Interactive tuner for the ESPHome chime custom component — detection via esphome/components/chime/core compiled to wasm/chime.js (emsdk 3.1.6, util/). Downsampled to 16 kHz · FFT 1024 · Hann · 50% overlap. Follow the 5 steps below to go from raw audio → deployed YAML.
Workflow 1 Upload + Gain 2 Spectral gates ◎ Spectrogram 3 Pattern 4 Simulate 5 Export scroll or click to navigate
1Upload audio & match microphone gain tune dB to emulate deployed ESP32 mic

Drop in a recording of your chime (WAV/MP3/FLAC/OGG/M4A/PCM). Use gain to make the spectrogram levels roughly match what your ESP32 microphone hears — this affects the segment detector and the chime simulator equally. Tip: generate a test signal from your current chime pattern when you have no recording yet. Or capture live audio from an ESPHome mic_streamer device below — the stream is recorded and then analysed like a file.

Mic stream idle
Streams 16-bit 16 kHz mono PCM (audio/L16; rate=16000; channels=1) from GET /mic_stream on your ESPHome mic_streamer device. Requires allow_without_auth: true so the browser can fetch it (device sends Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *). One client at a time — the device replies 429 if already streaming. The stream auto-finalises when the device closes it (max_duration) or when you press Stop & analyse. Raw .pcm captures can also be uploaded via the file input.
2Tune spectral gates isolate the desired chords from the sample

Adjust the gates below until the highlighted segments in the spectrogram (and the table) contain only the tones you care about. These gates are a helper for visual isolation and for importing into the simulator — the real detector uses the chime parameters in Step 4.

4Tune simulator parameters only detect desired pattern, ignore other noise

Adjust the global detector parameters and per-chime gates (threshold, SNR, prominence, onset, tail) then Run detection over the whole sample. Green bars = sensor active, ✓ = detection, red = rejected/timeout. Click the strip, chart, or events to jump to that time and inspect the per-bin diagnostics below.
